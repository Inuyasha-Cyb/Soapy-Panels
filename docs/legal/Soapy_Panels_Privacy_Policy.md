# Soapy Panels Privacy Notice

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Status: Draft for owner/legal approval before public release.

## Summary

Soapy Panels is an offline desktop application. The application does not
require an account and does not operate a Soapy Panels cloud service. The
current version does not include analytics, telemetry, crash-report uploads,
behavioral advertising, or automatic transfer of project content to Soapy
Panels.

## Content you create or import

Project files, imported images, custom stickers, custom fonts, and exports are
processed on your device. Soapy Panels accesses a file only when you select it
or when the application needs it to perform an action you requested. Saved
`.soapy` projects and exported PNG, JPEG, GIF, or MP4 files go to locations you
choose.

Soapy Panels does not upload this content. You control its retention by
keeping, moving, or deleting files through your operating system.

## Windows Store edition

The Windows Store edition can offer a lifetime remove-ads entitlement and a
monthly Soapy Plus entitlement. Microsoft Store processes purchases, billing,
refunds, subscriptions, and account information under Microsoft's own terms
and privacy practices. Soapy Panels does not receive full payment-card
details.

To determine which official Windows features to enable, the application may
request product and entitlement status from Microsoft Store. It may keep a
limited entitlement cache in the application's local user-data directory so a
recently verified entitlement can continue to work when Store services are
temporarily unavailable. That cache contains entitlement state and timing
information, not payment-card data.

The Windows Store edition may display promotional images bundled inside the
application. In this version, those images are local files. The application
does not contact an advertising network, load tracking pixels, or send ad-view
events to Soapy Panels.

## Linux Community edition

The Linux Community edition is fully unlocked, with no advertising or
in-application purchases.

## Support communications

If you email Soapy.Panels@outlook.com, your message and any information you
choose to include are handled by the email providers involved. That
information is used to respond to your request, investigate an issue, or act
on your feedback. Avoid sending confidential project content unless it is
necessary and you intend to share it.

## Operating-system and distribution services

Your operating system, Microsoft Store, Linux distribution tools, download
host, or code-hosting service may independently process technical or account
information. Their practices are controlled by their own policies, not this
notice.

## Children

Soapy Panels does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Because the application has no account or hosted data service, it does not ask
users to provide an age or profile.

## Security

No software can guarantee absolute security. Keep your operating system and
application current, obtain builds from a source you trust, and protect files
that contain sensitive information.

## Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated if the application's data practices change. A new
effective date will appear at the top. Material new online data collection
should be described before it is enabled in an official build.

## Contact

Questions about this notice may be sent to Soapy.Panels@outlook.com.
