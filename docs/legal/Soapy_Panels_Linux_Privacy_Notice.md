# Soapy Panels for Linux Privacy Notice

Draft date: November 25, 2026

Status: Draft for owner/legal approval before public release.

## Our commitment

Official Soapy Panels for Linux releases do not collect or transmit user
data to the developer or other recipients. Future official Soapy Panels for Linux
releases will maintain this commitment. The application has no analytics,
telemetry, tracking, advertising, account system, automatic crash-report
uploads, or automatic project uploads. It does not sell or share user data.

Soapy Panels for Linux is an open-source, offline desktop editor. Your
artwork is processed on your device. You do not need an account or an internet
connection to edit, save, or export your work. All features are
available without purchases or subscriptions.

## Files and settings on your device

The application reads and processes the images, text, fonts, stickers, and
projects you choose to use. It saves projects and exported images or videos to
locations you select. It also stores application settings and imported custom
fonts and stickers locally so they are available the next time you open it.

Local recovery copies may contain your unsaved artwork and imported content.
They help recover work after an interruption. Temporary export files and
application caches may also exist on your device; interrupted cleanup can
leave files for a later cleanup attempt. If diagnostic logging is explicitly
enabled, diagnostic messages are written locally. These local files are not
uploaded to Soapy Panels.

## Managing local data

Use Help > Manage recovery copies to inspect and remove recovery copies that
the application permits you to manage. Save important work before removing a
copy. Custom assets can be removed through their management controls. You can
move or delete saved projects and exports using your file manager.

Deleting the AppImage does not delete your projects, exports, or application
data. To remove all local application data, close Soapy Panels and remove its
user-data directory after backing up anything you want to keep. This is
normally the Soapy Panels or soapy-panels application directory beneath
`$XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, or beneath `~/.config` when that variable is unset. A
custom launch profile can use another location. Diagnostic logs, when enabled,
may be written separately beneath the directory from which the app was
started. Keep files you wish to retain before deleting any of these folders.

Files stored in a folder synchronized by another application may be uploaded
by that application. Soapy Panels does not control your backup or sync tools.

## Information you choose to send separately

If you email Soapy.Panels@outlook.com or submit an issue, comment, or attachment
on GitHub, you choose what information to send. The project can receive and
use those messages to respond, investigate an issue, or act on feedback.
Email and hosting providers handle the messages under their own policies;
public issues and attachments may be visible to anyone. Do not include private
artwork or personal information unless you intend to share it.

Optional donation links open an external website in your browser only when
you choose to follow them. Those websites handle their own accounts, payments,
and privacy practices. Soapy Panels does not automatically attach your
projects or diagnostic files to these actions.

## Other software and distributions

Your operating system, download host, browser, and other services have their
own practices. This notice describes the official Soapy Panels for Linux application,
not those independent services. Modified or unofficial builds are controlled
by their distributors; inspect their source and privacy statements before
relying on them. Our commitment applies to official Soapy Panels for Linux releases.

## Security and future revisions

Local processing does not guarantee absolute security. Protect your device,
obtain builds from a source you trust, and keep backups of important work.

This notice may be clarified or corrected as documentation improves. Such
revisions will preserve the commitment that official Soapy Panels for Linux releases
do not collect or transmit user data. They will not authorize analytics,
tracking, or other automatic data collection in future official releases.
